## About Pangram Solver

Pangram Solver helps you find pangrams and reveal hints for seven-letter Spelling Bee word puzzles. It runs in your browser using a bundled dictionary. Solving works offline and requires no account.

## Data stored on your device

Pangram Solver keeps your current puzzle’s seven letter positions, your Full Answers or Hints Only preference, and the individual answers you chose to reveal. A schema version and a save revision help manage the record. This data stays in local extension storage in your browser profile. Your puzzle and preferences can therefore be restored when you close the popup or restart the browser. The extension regenerates the matching list when needed and keeps no puzzle history.

Use Reset to clear your puzzle letters and individual reveals. Reset keeps your selected display mode and updates the local save record. You can remove the remaining local extension data by uninstalling Pangram Solver or clearing its extension data or browser profile. If the browser cannot save a change, Pangram Solver displays a storage warning.

## Network and optional website visit

Dunedin Studio does not receive your puzzle, preference, or reveal data through the extension. The extension uses no analytics, advertising, or account service. Its dictionary and solving logic are included in the installation.

The footer contains an optional link to SpellingBeeAnswers.app. If you select it, your browser opens the homepage in a new tab. The extension sends no puzzle letters, reveal data, or tracking parameters in the link. The separate website has its own privacy practices.

## Children’s privacy

Pangram Solver does not ask for names, email addresses, or other personal details. Dunedin Studio does not collect personal information from children through this extension.

## Changes and contact

Dunedin Studio will update this policy and its effective date if Pangram Solver’s data practices change. For support or privacy questions, use the [Dunedin Studio contact page](https://spellingbeeanswers.app/contact).
