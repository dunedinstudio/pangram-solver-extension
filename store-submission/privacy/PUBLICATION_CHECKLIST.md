# Privacy-policy publication checklist

1. Review the [Pangram Solver policy draft](PANGRAM_SOLVER_PRIVACY_POLICY.md), confirm the publisher and contact destination, and replace the effective-date placeholder.
2. Publish the dedicated policy under an owner-controlled HTTPS URL. Proposed path on spellingbeeanswers.app is `/pangram-solver-privacy-policy`. Record the final URL after publication.
3. Open the final URL in a signed-out Chrome and Edge window. Confirm HTTP 200, no login requirement, readable text, the exact extension name, the effective date, the local-save description, and a working contact link. Check the policy’s link independently from both dashboards before submitting.
4. Enter the verified URL in the privacy fields for Chrome and Edge. Update the policy and declarations before shipping a future build that changes data handling.

The proposed page is awaiting owner publication. The existing Spelling Bee Solver policy covers that product.
