## Full description

Pangram Solver helps you find pangrams in seven-letter Spelling Bee word games. Put the required center letter in the yellow hexagon, then enter the six other letters. A pangram uses all seven letters, including the center. The extension checks the combination against its packaged dictionary.

Start in Hints Only to see each pangram’s first two letters and total length. Use the eye beside an entry to reveal it when you’re ready. The progress count keeps track of your reveals. Switch to Full Answers for the alphabetical list of all matches.

Your current puzzle, display mode, and individually revealed words are available when you reopen the popup. Use Reset to clear the puzzle and reveals while keeping the selected mode. The extension is free, works offline, and needs no account.

Pangram Solver is independently published by Dunedin Studio. Its results are dictionary candidates, and the words accepted by your game may differ.
