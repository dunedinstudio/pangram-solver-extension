## Detailed description

Find pangrams and get hints for seven-letter Spelling Bee word puzzles. Enter the required center letter in the yellow hexagon and the six different outer letters around it. Pangram Solver looks for dictionary words that use all seven letters, including the center.

Hints Only is selected when you first install the extension. Each hint shows the first two letters and the word length. Select the eye beside a hint to reveal that pangram and keep the others covered. Choose Full Answers to see all matching pangrams in alphabetical order.

Close the popup to return to your game. When you reopen it, your puzzle, mode, and individual reveals are restored. Reset clears the letters and reveals while keeping your selected mode.

Pangram Solver is free, works offline, and requires no account. It is an independent tool for Spelling Bee games. Candidates come from a bundled dictionary. Individual games may accept different words.
